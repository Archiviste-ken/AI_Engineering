Classical Machine Learning (Our Project)

We don't use tokenizers or embeddings.

Instead, we use something like:

Sentence

↓

Split into words

↓

Count words

↓

Vector of numbers

For example:

Vocabulary:

win
free
call
meeting
money

Message:

"Free money"

Becomes:

win	free	call	meeting	money
0	1	0	0	1

Now the model can understand it.

This technique is called Bag of Words.

Later, we'll learn TF-IDF, which is a smarter version.\
