# Customer Churn Prediction

## Business Problem

Telecom customers may leave the service without enough early warning for the retention team to intervene. The business needs a way to identify customers who are at risk of churning so targeted action can be taken before they leave.

## Objective

Build a machine learning model that predicts whether a customer is likely to churn based on historical customer information.

## Machine Learning Problem

This is a binary classification problem. The model should learn patterns from past customer behavior and predict whether a customer will churn or stay.

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if the model can reliably identify at-risk customers early enough to support retention efforts, with strong classification performance on unseen data.
